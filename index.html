Research Engineer · BioAI · InstaDeep

Vincent O’Luasa

An engineer trained in control systems and robotics, now working where deep learning meets biology.

At InstaDeep’s BioAI department I train inverse-folding models from scratch, fine-tune protein-folding models to predict binding affinity, and study model latent spaces to understand how a single point mutation changes a protein.

Before that, I led an R&D team working on generative image models, action recognition and object segmentation, and delivered data platforms for industry clients. My path runs through Paris and Madrid, from classical mechanics to computer vision to proteins.

Outside of work I follow two frontiers closely: the cosmos and the brain. I studied neuroscience through HarvardX and am currently exploring quantum physics.
