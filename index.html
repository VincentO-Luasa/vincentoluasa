My job in 30 seconds

  1. T cells patrol your body

    Each T cell carries a receptor (the TCR) that inspects small protein fragments, peptides, displayed by MHC molecules on the surface of every cell.

  2. Cancer cells show abnormal peptides

    But natural TCRs often bind them too weakly to trigger an attack, so the tumour slips through.

  3. One mutation can change everything

    Changing a single amino acid in the TCR’s binding loops can make it grip much harder, or let go. With 20 amino acids per position, the space of possible variants explodes.

  4. My models predict which ones work

    I train deep-learning models to predict how each mutation changes TCR–pMHC binding, so the lab tests the most promising candidates first. Try it yourself →
